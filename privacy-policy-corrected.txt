2. Information We Collect
HPClean does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect personally identifiable information ("PII") such as your name, phone number, physical address, or government-issued identification.
To provide and improve our services, we may collect certain non-personally identifiable information as described below.
Device Information
When you access or use the App, we may receive certain device-related information, including:
* Device model
* Operating system version
* Device language settings
* App version
* Device identifiers
This information is collected in an aggregated and non-identifiable manner and is not associated with any specific individual.
App Usage Information
To provide storage cleanup functionality, the App may access information stored on your device, including:
* Photos
* Videos
* Screenshots
* Contact information (when applicable and authorized)
* Calendar events (when applicable and authorized)
We do not upload, store, or process the actual content of your media files on our servers. All analysis is performed locally on your device unless otherwise specified.
Media Metadata
The App may access metadata associated with photos and videos, including:
* Creation date
* File size
* Resolution
* Video duration
* Media type
* Local resource identifiers
This information is used solely for:
* Sorting and organizing media
* Detecting duplicate or similar files
* Generating storage statistics
* Providing cleanup recommendations
Photo Location Metadata
If photos contain embedded location metadata, the App may read this information and use Apple's geocoding services to display location names such as cities, regions, or countries.
The App does not request or track your real-time location.
Cleanup Statistics
The App stores certain statistics locally on your device, including:
* Number of deleted photos
* Number of deleted videos
* Estimated storage space freed
These statistics are used exclusively for displaying information within the App and managing feature limitations.
Notification Permission Status
If you grant notification permissions, the App may send local reminders to help you manage and clean your device storage.
These notifications are generated locally and are not delivered through our servers.
Subscription Information
The App uses Apple StoreKit to:
* Display subscription products
* Process purchases
* Restore previous purchases
* Verify subscription status
All payment transactions are handled directly by Apple.
We do not collect, process, or store:
* Credit card numbers
* Bank account information
* Payment credentials
Email Address
If required for payment confirmation or customer support, we may collect your email address to:
* Send purchase confirmations
* Respond to support inquiries
* Verify Premium access
Advertising Information
The current version of the App does not display advertisements and does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

4. Local Processing of Media
Most media analysis is performed entirely on your device.
This includes:
* Similar photo detection
* Duplicate photo grouping
* Screenshot classification
* Video categorization
* Storage estimation
* Cleanup recommendations
The App utilizes local algorithms and OpenCV technologies to perform these functions without uploading your media content to external servers.
________________
5. Third-Party Services
Certain features rely on Apple or third-party services:
iCloud Photos
When iCloud Photos is enabled, Apple's Photos framework may retrieve media from iCloud for display or playback.
Apple StoreKit
Apple manages:
* Purchases
* Subscriptions
* Billing
* Refunds
* Purchase restoration
Apple Geocoding Services
Location metadata embedded within photos may be converted into human-readable place names using Apple's geocoding services.
iOS Notification Services
Local notifications are managed and delivered by the iOS operating system.

Please note that deleting the App does not automatically cancel an active subscription.
Subscriptions must be managed through your Apple ID account settings.
